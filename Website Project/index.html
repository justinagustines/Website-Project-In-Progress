Why Picking Up Trash Matters in Saipan

Protect Coral Reefs

Plastics and runoff wash into the Saipan beaches, smothering coral and harming marine life like sea turtles and reef fish.

Preserve Island Beauty

Clean beaches, trails, and villages keep our home healthy for local families and welcoming for visitors.

Stop Storm Drain Pollution

Litter left on roadsides in Chalan Kanoa, Susupe, or Koblerville gets carried straight into the ocean during heavy rains or typhoons.
