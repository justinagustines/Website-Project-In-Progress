Cleanup Areas Around Saipan

Help us keep these popular beaches, villages, and scenic areas litter-free.

Micro Beach & Garapan

Focus: Single-use plastics, bottle caps, and fishing lines, and aluminum cans along the shoreline and parks.

Ladder Beach & Obyan

Focus: Picnic waste, aluminum cans, and coastal debris washed up along the southern coast.

Banzai Cliff & Marpi

Focus: Roadside litter and windblown trash along the cliffs and historical sites.

Take Initiative with these three words

THROW THESE TRASH

Whenever you visit the beach or walk through your village, pick up trash and dispose of them properly within these locations.
